spread, the cache-hit vs full-rebuild split. Whole-run durations are bimodal
the same way (median 8.8m, min 5.5m, max 134.6m). So weights give honest
movement at step BOUNDARIES, but interpolating inside the build step needs a
cap so the number cannot creep past that step's band on a long build.

with 110 minutes left.
